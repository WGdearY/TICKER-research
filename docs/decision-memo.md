# NIKE Investment Committee Decision Memo

**Decision:** Watch/defer. Do not open a new 12-month NIKE position at $38.24 per share, the September 2, 2026 close. The supported valuation range is **$30-$49 per share**. The committee should reconsider at or below $36.40, or after reported results support the required revenue and margin recovery.

## Decision evidence and as-of date

The intended user is an investment committee with no existing NIKE position. The primary source boundary is NIKE's FY2026 Form 10-K, filed July 15, 2026 for the year ended May 31, 2026. Market data ends at the September 2, 2026 close of $38.24; no later information is used.

NIKE finished FY2026 with $46.398 billion of revenue, $3.850 billion of EBIT, and $3.108 billion of net income. Cash and short-term investments were $9.027 billion, compared with $7.942 billion of book debt, producing $1.085 billion of net cash. The balance sheet provides resilience, but the operating evidence does not yet establish a broad turnaround: consolidated currency-neutral revenue fell 2%, NIKE Direct fell 8%, and Greater China fell 13%.

The range deliberately preserves disagreement among methods rather than averaging them. The linked FCFE pro-forma produces $30.19 per diluted share in its base case; its terminal value represents 79.9% of total equity value. The earlier full-recovery FCFF case produces $48.53 per diluted share but requires EBIT margin expansion from about 8.3% to 12.0%. Trading-comps EV/EBITDA implies $45.55 at the peer median but spans $27.55-$138.77 because the peer set includes both distressed and high-growth companies. The credible overlap is therefore a $30-$49 decision range, not a single target price.

## Material assumptions and sensitivities

The FCFE base case assumes FY2027-FY2031 revenue growth of 3%, 4%, 5%, 4%, and 3%; gross margin rises from 43.2% to 44.5%; capex is 1.5% of revenue; cost of equity is 9.0%; and terminal growth is 2.5%. These are analyst assumptions, not management guidance.

The accounting-linked sensitivity suite passed the balance-sheet check in every run and restored the base inputs after testing. A +/-1 percentage-point revenue-growth change moves value from $29.62 to $30.76 per share; a +/-1 percentage-point gross-margin change moves it from $29.12 to $31.26; and changing capex from 1% to 2% of revenue moves it from $32.60 to $27.79. Capex has the largest tested value span ($4.81 per share). The results confirm that cash conversion and reinvestment are material to the lower end of the range.

## Risks, triggers, and committee ask

The thesis fails if NIKE Direct and Greater China do not stabilize, promotional discounting delays margin recovery, or future evidence makes the terminal-value dependence less credible. Monitor quarterly revenue by channel and geography, gross margin, EBIT margin, inventory, and promotional commentary through 10-Qs and earnings releases. Re-run the range after each report.

**Committee ask:** Maintain no position today. Authorize monitoring only. Reconsider an initiation if (1) the price is at or below $36.40, or (2) filed results show that revenue and margin recovery are occurring. See `validation-and-ai-use.md` for the full source, test, and AI-use record.
