## What changed from Edition A to Edition B and why

The action did not materially change: both editions support watch/defer. The evidence became more specific and more constrained. The initial rough upside/downside bands became a $30-$49 range after linked DCF work, a peer-policy-based EV/EBITDA cross-check, and a reverse-DCF comparison. The most important qualification is that neither the $48.53 recovery case nor the $45.55 peer-median result independently justifies an initiation: each relies on conditions that have not yet been reported. The lower $30.19 FCFE base case and the sensitivity results demonstrate why the committee requires a price or evidence trigger.

AI was used after Edition A to organize sources, structure drafts, and suggest calculation checks. The cited filing, arithmetic, and model outputs were independently checked and are documented in `validation-and-ai-use.md`.
