My recommendation is watch/defer. Do not initiate a new NIKE position at the September 2 close of $38.24. I support a $30 to $49 per-share range, not a single price target. I am asking the committee to authorize monitoring only and to reconsider an initiation at $36.40 or below, or after reported revenue and operating-margin recovery.

The valuation range reflects three distinct views. The linked FCFE base case is $30.19 per share. It incorporates 3% to 5% revenue growth, gross margin rising to 44.5%, 1.5% capex as a percent of revenue, 9% cost of equity, and 2.5% terminal growth. The full-recovery FCFF case is $48.53, but it assumes EBIT margin reaches 12%. Comparable-company EV/EBITDA produces a $45.55 median, but the peer dispersion is too large to treat it as a precise answer. I do not average these methods.

The question is whether the recovery is evidenced today. It is not. FY2026 revenue was flat reported and down 2% currency-neutral. NIKE Direct was down 8% currency-neutral and Greater China was down 13%. NIKE does have $1.085 billion of net cash, so this is not a distress decision; it is a decision about whether the price compensates us for waiting through an uncertain turnaround.

The most material model risk is cash conversion. In the sensitivity suite, a capex change from 1% to 2% of revenue moves per-share value from $32.60 to $27.79. That range is why a margin-recovery story alone is not enough. The model is accounting-linked, every scenario balances, and a forced balance-sheet error is rejected rather than valued.

My reversal and monitoring rules are explicit. Do not initiate unless the price is at or below $36.40 or the company reports enough evidence to support the modelled recovery. Monitor quarterly revenue by channel and geography, gross margin, EBIT margin, inventory, and promotion-related commentary. If NIKE Direct or Greater China deteriorates further, or margin recovery fails to appear, hold the watch/defer stance and rerun the valuation range.

My committee ask is no position today and approval to monitor these triggers.
