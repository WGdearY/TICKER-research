## Speech script

**[Start with Terminal visible; press Enter to run the command.]**

Good afternoon. This live model output supports my recommendation: Watch/Defer on NIKE. Do not initiate a new position at the September 2, 2026 closing price of $38.24. My supported value range is $30 to $49 per share. I am asking the committee to authorize monitoring only and reconsider an initiation at $36.40 or below, or after reported revenue and operating-margin recovery.

**[Open the JSON output, then the decision memo.]**

The range reflects three different valuation views. The linked FCFE base case is $30.19 per share. The full-recovery FCFF case is $48.53, but it requires EBIT margin to recover to 12 percent. Comparable-company EV-to-EBITDA gives a $45.55 median, but the peer range is too dispersed to treat as a precise answer. I do not average these methods, so I use a range rather than a single target price.

The recovery is not evidenced today. FY2026 revenue was flat on a reported basis and down 2 percent currency-neutral. NIKE Direct was down 8 percent currency-neutral, and Greater China was down 13 percent. NIKE has $1.085 billion of net cash, so this is not a distress decision. It is a decision about whether the current price compensates us for waiting through an uncertain turnaround.

The main risk is cash conversion. Changing capex from 1 to 2 percent of revenue moves value from $32.60 to $27.79 per share. Margin recovery alone is therefore not enough to support an investment.

I would reconsider an initiation if the price is at or below $36.40, or if filed results show revenue and operating-margin recovery. Each quarter, I will monitor revenue by channel and geography, gross margin, EBIT margin, inventory, and promotion-related commentary.

My committee ask is no position today and approval to monitor these triggers. Thank you.
