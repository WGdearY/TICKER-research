## Is NIKE growing?

**Conclusion: not at the consolidated, currency-neutral FY2026 level.** NIKE’s revenue was flat reported and down 2% currency-neutral, while NIKE Brand was down 1% currency-neutral. [S3] There are specific signs of improvement in North American wholesale, but they are offset by declining NIKE Direct, Greater China, and Converse results. [S3][S4][S5] It would be unsupported to label NIKE broadly "growing" solely from the FY2026 MD&A. This conclusion does not rule out a future turnaround; such a prediction is **Unsourced** without later reported results.
