## 1. Launch: Predict-Before-Run Habit (Training Case)

### The Stated Prediction
* **Experiment:** Hold all training-case cash flows and inputs constant ($\text{FCFF}_0 = 100$, growth $8/6/5/4/3\%$, $g = 3\%$, cash $50$, debt $300$, $50\text{M}$ shares), and increase WACC from $10.0\%$ to $11.0\%$ ($+100\text{ bps}$).
* **Written Prediction Before Run:** Value per share will fall from the base of **$27.50** to approximately **$23.50** (a decrease of roughly $15\%$). Direction is unambiguously negative because all five annual discount factors widen and the perpetual growth denominator expands.

### Actual Engine Result
* **Base Value ($10\%$ WACC, $3\%$ g):** **$27.50** ($27.4974$)
* **Stressed Value ($11\%$ WACC, $3\%$ g):** **$23.41** ($23.4079$)
* **Percentage Change:** **$-14.87\%$ ($\approx -15\%$)**

### Professional Interpretation
The terminal value denominator is $(WACC - g)$. At $10\%$ WACC and $3\%$ growth, that spread is $7\text{ cents}$ ($0.07$). When WACC increases by just 1 point to $11\%$, the spread widens to $8\text{ cents}$ ($0.08$)—a **$+14.3\%$ expansion** in the divisor. Combined with greater discounting across the five explicit forecast years, the resulting equity value drops by nearly $15\%$.  
**Core Committee Takeaway:** A DCF valuation is hostage to its discount rate. Presenting a single point estimate to an investment committee conveys false precision; robust valuation requires presenting a defended range with explicit sensitivity boundaries.

### Monotonicity & Boundary Verification
1. **Row Monotonicity ($\frac{\partial V}{\partial \text{WACC}} < 0$):** **PASS**. Holding perpetual growth $g$ constant at any column, moving down rows (increasing WACC) strictly decreases per-share value across all 25 cells.
2. **Column Monotonicity ($\frac{\partial V}{\partial g} > 0$):** **PASS**. Holding WACC constant across any row, moving right columns (increasing $g$) strictly increases per-share value across all 25 cells.
3. **Economic Boundary Check ($g < \text{WACC}$):** **PASS**. The Gordon Growth denominator remains strictly positive throughout ($5.00\%$ to $8.00\%$), preventing asymptotic division by zero.

## 3. Reverse DCF: Decoding the Market-Implied Expectations

### Mechanics and Solved Parameters
Reverse DCF fixes all explicit turnaround cash flows and solves for the perpetual growth rate $g$ required to make the DCF value exactly equal observed market prices:

* **Observed Regular Close (September 2, 2026):** **$38.24** [S2]
  * **Solved Implied Perpetual Growth ($g$):** **$0.1549\%$ ($\approx 0.15\%$)**
* **Observed Regular Close (August 31, 2026):** **$39.06** [S2]
  * **Solved Implied Perpetual Growth ($g$):** **$0.4021\%$ ($\approx 0.40\%$)**
* **Student Reconsideration Trigger Price:** **$36.40** [U1]
  * **Solved Implied Perpetual Growth ($g$):** **$-0.4551\%$ ($\approx -0.46\%$)**

### Economic Interpretation of Reverse DCF
* **What the Market is Saying:** Long-term mature companies in the U.S. economy generally grow at $2.0\%\text{--}3.0\%$ (nominal GDP trend). An implied growth rate of **$0.15\%$** does *not* mean the market expects Nike to survive forever with zero inflation adjustments.
* **The Real Market Disconnect:** The market is signaling **profound disbelief in the explicit forecast**. At $\$38.24$, investors do not believe Nike can expand EBIT margins from $8.3\%$ to $12.0\%$ by FY2031 while growing revenue at $3.8\%$ annually. If that operational recovery fails and margins remain near $9.5\%$, the fair value drops directly to **$28.76**.

## 4. AI Bull / Bear Stress-Test Challenge

An independent dual-system AI critique (ChatGPT & Gemini) was run to challenge the base thesis:

### AI Bull Counter-Argument
> *"NIKE’s North American wholesale revenue grew 14% currency-neutral in FY2026, demonstrating that retail partners are restocking and brand equity is intact. Net cash of $1.085B and annual operating cash flow of $2.87B ensure dividend and reinvestment safety. The market price of $38.24 is 21% below base DCF ($48.53), creating an asymmetric risk/reward to initiate now."*

### AI Bear Attack
> *"Turnaround assumptions are aggressive and unproven. FY2026 consolidated revenue fell 2% currency-neutral, Greater China fell 13% currency-neutral with digital sales collapsing 29%, and Converse plunged 31%. Direct-to-consumer digital traffic is shrinking. Valuing NIKE at $48.53 relies on 76% terminal value and an unevidenced 370 bps margin recovery."*

### Independent Verification of One Specific Claim
* **Claim Tested:** The Bear statement that *Greater China revenue fell 13% currency-neutral with digital sales collapsing 29%*.
* **Primary Source Verification:** Checked against **NIKE FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A (p. 38)** [S5].
  * *Filing Evidence:* Greater China wholesale was down $14\%$ currency-neutral, Direct was down $12\%$, digital was down $29\%$, and total territory revenue fell from $\$7.248\text{B}$ to $\$6.578\text{B}$ ($-9\%$ reported, **$-13\%$ currency-neutral**).
* **Disposition:** **Verified as factual.** The bear critique is factually justified by SEC reporting. Because the turnaround is not yet visible in reported numbers, initiating today would violate professional margin of safety rules.
