## Decision and committee ask

**Company / ticker:** NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE)

**Valuation date:** September 1, 2026, using the last completed regular-session close: August 31, 2026.

**Decision:** **Watch/defer.** The base DCF indicates value above the last completed market close, but the margin of safety is insufficient for a new 12-month position given the turnaround-dependent forecast and terminal-value concentration.

**Committee action requested:** Do not open a position today. Reconsider at or below $36.40 per share (a 25% discount to the base-case DCF value) or after evidence supports the modeled revenue and margin recovery.

## Executive view

NIKE has a strong global brand and ended FY2026 with $1.085 billion of net cash using cash, short-term investments, and book debt. The base DCF produces a $48.53 per-share value, 24.3% above the last completed close of $39.06. However, the valuation relies on operating-margin expansion from 8.2% in FY2026 to 12.0% in FY2031 and 76% of enterprise value comes from the terminal value. The committee should watch rather than initiate until either the price provides a 25% margin of safety or reported results substantiate the recovery.
